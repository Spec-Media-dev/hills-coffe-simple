## Overall judgement

The existing cream/green/gold palette, documentary coffee imagery and B2B subject matter are a sound foundation. The main issue is **sequence and repetition**, not the need for a completely different identity. Large intent rows, a sourcing story and an abstract proof block postpone actual coffees. Multiple full-height story/conversion sections then repeat the promise. Use the references to make the page calmer and more decisive, not to recreate seven different brands.

Keep Dubai as the brand origin and Egypt as an operational presence. The public journey remains intent → evaluate a coffee/evidence → RFQ or sample → commercial conversation → allocation → delivery. Public browsing and account-gated pricing are distinct from an authorized trading portal. No retail cart, anonymous marketplace, leverage, futures or speculative trading proposition belongs in this redesign.

### Recommended future order (not implemented)

Header → Hero → compact three intent cards → Coffee Offers with small account/pricing note → Origins → consolidated Traceability / Quality / sourcing proof → Supply / Logistics → Knowledge → RFQ / Sample CTA → Footer.

P0 = buyer understanding, evidence or conversion structure. P1 = meaningful visual/content refinement. P2 = optional polish or secondary-priority simplification. KEEP = no redesign needed.
